A WINDKEEPER'S TALE

Make a home
before the frost.

The forest is getting cold. Guide falling leaves into nests and hollows, and tuck every little creature into a warm autumn blanket.

Hold and drag anywhere to aim the wind. A longer arrow makes a stronger breeze. Release to let the leaves fall.

Or use arrow keys / WASD. Space pauses. Fill every shelter before the frost arrives.
